# Intro Ideas — "From the Editor"

A running scratchpad for the top-of-issue note — the one part of the newsletter
that's all you, not AI. Dump anything here: a thought, a thing you tried, a line
you overheard, a half-formed opinion. Steal from it when it's time to write.

**This file is just for you.** It is NOT read by the pipeline and does NOT affect
the generated newsletter (unlike the "Style guidance" section of `FEEDBACK.md`,
which is). Write freely.

You can edit this straight from GitHub on your phone (open the file → pencil
icon) if an idea hits when you're away from your laptop.

---

## Intro modes (for when you're staring at a blank cursor)

- **I tried this** — a real thing you did with AI lately, a win *or* a faceplant.
- **Honest skeptic** — something overhyped you're not buying, or a limit you hit.
- **Overheard** — a real question someone asked you about AI (a client, a friend, your barber).
- **Behind the curtain** — why *this* issue's tip is the hero, or what surprised you putting it together.
- **Zoom-out** — one genuine human reaction to a big story, in plain terms (use sparingly).

Guardrails: 2–4 sentences, one idea, sign with your name, and never a second tip.
If it could've been written by anyone about anything, cut it.
